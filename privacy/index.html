About this test of Beacon

Beacon is a reading practice tool for children. This is an early test version, made by Matova, Phoenix, AZ. You can reach us at ian@matovagroup.com.

What it does.

Your child reads short texts, answers eight questions, and picks interests so the next texts feel more fun. You see a report with the score, how long each question took, whether your child left the app, and whether they copied or pasted anything.

What we collect.

Nothing. This page does not send anything to us or to anyone else. The nickname you type (use a nickname, not a full name), your child's grade, interests and results are saved only in this browser, on this device. We cannot see them. In the Grown-up area there is a Copy summary button. It shows you the exact text first, with no name in it, and copies it only when you tap Copy. Nothing is sent. If you choose to send that text to us, you do that yourself.

What the report cannot tell you.

It is a signal, not proof. A phone call, a notification or a sibling can cause what it shows. Beacon cannot see other devices, books or a person sitting beside your child. Beacon does not read what is on the clipboard. It records that a paste happened and how long it was, not what it said.

Your control.

You can clear everything at any time in the Grown-up area, under Settings and privacy. Clearing removes the data from this device. We keep no copy.

The website host.

The service that hosts this page may keep standard visit logs, such as the time, the network address that asked for the page and the type of browser. We do not use these logs for anything about your child.

This is a test.

It is free. Nothing is sold. It may contain mistakes. It does not replace a teacher or a tutor, and it makes no promise about grades or test scores.

By ticking the box you confirm you are the child's parent or guardian and you agree that your child can try Beacon on this device.

Notice version T-2026-10-10.

This page sets no cookies of its own. It saves your child's results in this browser's storage, which you can clear.
